ClipAgent

A personal publishing tool

ClipAgent is a small desktop tool that its operator runs on their own computer to cut short vertical clips and upload them to social media accounts the operator owns: YouTube channels (through the YouTube Data API) and Instagram professional accounts (through the Instagram API).

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and it does not access anyone else's account. It posts only to accounts its operator has connected by signing in themselves.

What it does with connected accounts

  • Uploads videos, with titles, descriptions and captions, to the connected YouTube channel or Instagram account.
  • Reads the connected channel or account name, to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Nothing else: it does not read messages, comments, followers or analytics, and it does not post anywhere else.
