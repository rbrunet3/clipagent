Data deletion

ClipAgent keeps no data on any server. The only data it holds are sign-in tokens, stored on its operator's own computer.

  1. Remove ClipAgent from your account: Google at myaccount.google.com/connections; Instagram in Settings, then Apps and websites.
  2. That makes the stored token useless. To also have it deleted from the operator's computer, open an issue; it is deleted within 30 days.
