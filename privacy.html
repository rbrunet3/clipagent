Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

ClipAgent is a personal tool run by its operator on their own computer to upload videos to social media accounts the operator owns. This policy explains what data it handles.

Data it accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): permission to upload videos to the connected channel (youtube.upload) and to read the channel's name (youtube.readonly).
  • Instagram (Instagram API): the account's ID and username (instagram_business_basic) and permission to publish posts (instagram_business_content_publish).

How it is stored and used

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are sent only to Google's and Meta's own APIs.
  • They are used only to upload the operator's videos and to confirm which account is connected.
  • No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • This website has no analytics, cookies or trackers.

Google and YouTube

ClipAgent uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. ClipAgent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: remove ClipAgent at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Instagram: Settings, then Apps and websites, then remove ClipAgent.
  • Stored tokens are deleted from the operator's computer when access is revoked or on request; see data deletion. Instagram tokens that are not refreshed expire after 60 days.
